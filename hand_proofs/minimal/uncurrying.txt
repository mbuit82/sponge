Theorem: |- (A -> (B -> C)) -> ((A & B) -> C)

Using deduction
Using deduction

Proof
1. |- A -> (B -> C) by Assumption
2. |- A & B by Assumption
3. |- (A & B) -> A by And-EL
4. |- (A & B) -> B by And-ER
5. |- A by Modus Ponens, 2 3
6. |- B by Modus Ponens, 2 4
7. |- B -> C by Modus Ponens, 5 1
8. |- C by Modus Ponens, 6 7
