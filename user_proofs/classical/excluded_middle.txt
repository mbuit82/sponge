Theorem: |- A v ~A

Proof
1. |- (~A & ~~A) -> ~A by And Elim L
2. |- (~A & ~~A) -> ~~A by And Elim R
3. |- ~(~A & ~~A) by Negation Intro: 1 2
4. |- ~(A v ~A) -> (~A & ~~A) by demorgans_neg_or (from intuitionistic)
5. |- ~(~A & ~~A) -> ~~(A v ~A) by contrapositive_forward (from intuitionistic): 4
6. |- ~~(A v ~A) by Modus Ponens: 3 5
7. |- A v ~A by DNE: 6
QED
