Theorem: |- (A -> B) -> (~A v B)


Using deduction

Proof
1. |- (A -> B) by Assumption
2. |- B -> (~A v B) by Or Intro R
3. |- A -> (~A v B) by hypothetical_syllogism (from minimal): 1 2
4. |- ~A -> (~A v B) by Or Intro L
5. |- (A v ~A) by excluded_middle (from classical)
6. |- ~A v B by Or Elim: 3 4 5
