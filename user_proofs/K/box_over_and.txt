Theorem: |- L(A & B) -> (LA & LB)




Proof
1. |- (A & B) -> A              by And Elim L
2. |- (A & B) -> B              by And Elim R
3. |- L(A & B) -> LA            by K reasoning: 1
4. |- L(A & B) -> LB            by K reasoning: 2
5. |- L(A & B) -> (LA & LB)     by applying adjunction_in_consequent (from minimal): 3 4
