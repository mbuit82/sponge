Theorem: |- LP -> LLP





Proof
1. |- P -> (LP -> (P & LP))                 by And Intro
2. |- (P & LP) -> P                         by And Elim L
3. |- L(P & LP) -> LP                       by K reasoning: 2
4. |- P -> (L(P & LP) -> (P & LP))          by weakening: 3 1
5. |- LP -> L(L(P & LP) -> (P & LP))        by K reasoning: 4
6. |- L(L(P & LP) -> (P & LP)) -> L(P & LP) by GL Axiom
7. |- LP -> L(P & LP)                       by applying hypothetical_syllogism (from minimal): 5 6
8. |- (P & LP) -> LP                        by And Elim R
9. |- L(P & LP) -> LLP                      by K reasoning: 8
10. |- LP -> LLP                            by applying hypothetical_syllogism (from minimal): 7 9
QED
(everything after QED is ignored)
