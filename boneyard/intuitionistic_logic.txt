Syntax
p | Bot | p -> p

Axioms
Axiom1: |- p -> p
Axiom2: |- p -> q -> p
Axiom3: |- (p -> q) -> (p -> q -> r) -> p -> r
Axiom4: |- (p -> Bot) -> p -> q

Inference Rules
Modus Ponens: |-p, |- p -> q, |- q

NOTE: I actually don't know what I want the front end to look like yet. So why not just not deal with it, and just do an intermediate rep with python dicts or smth? Because from the front end to the back end is really two things: readability and abstraction. The _content_ is abstracted, but also the style is more readable. Those are two tasks. Of course readability is subjective and changes from any level to any other, but readability need not be a primary concern of an abstraction layer—the readability layer can sit on top to make the entire system more modular.
